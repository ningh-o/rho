are **regenerated from the new seed** — never hand-written. Programs
are adapted, not copied verbatim, wherever the new law changed
behavior; a program's adaptation notes live in a header comment.

## Order

Tranches follow boot milestones: tranche 1 lands with T1.10 (checker +
emit + prelude complete), tranche 2 with the first gate leg (T3.1
prep), tranche 3 alongside (its programs are the conformance-map
anchors for the new rules). Every golden is regenerated by running the
freshly built seed; the harness records stdout + exit, and those
recorded files are the goldens thereafter (D1 determinism keeps them
stable).
